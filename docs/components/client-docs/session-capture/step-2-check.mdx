A mask that does not apply fails quietly: a misspelled class records the text as it is. Look at a real replay of every page that shows private data before you ship it:

1. In a development build, add `recordAllSessions: true` to the options, so every session streams its replay.
2. Open the pages with private data and type into their fields. Then switch to another tab, which uploads the segment in progress.
3. In Traceway, open **Sessions**, open your session and play it. Masked text shows as `*`, blank elements are empty boxes, and typed values are `*`.

Remove `recordAllSessions` before you ship. Check again after a change to those pages; to check in production, capture your own browser ([Step 5](#step-5-capture-one-user-or-device-from-the-dashboard)).
