A capture makes one browser upload what it buffered, then stream everything, replay included, for up to 24 hours. It targets a device, a user, a session or any attribute. To capture a user, the SDK has to know who they are: set `user.id` after sign-in, then ask for the settings at once, so a capture or a rule for that user applies without waiting for the next poll:

{props.children}

Every browser profile also has a device id, `service.instance.id`, which `getInstanceId()` returns and every session shows among its attributes. A support page can display it for the user to read out.

Then, in Traceway:

1. Open the user's session and click **Capture this device**, or click **Capture device** on an issue's event. Otherwise open **Data Collection** in the project's sidebar and click **New Capture** under **Captures**.
2. Choose the **Target**: **Device (instance id)**, **User (user.id)**, **Session** or **Attribute**, and enter its **Value**.
3. Choose how long it runs (**For**, up to 24 hours) and what it uploads first (**Include**: nothing earlier, the last minute, the last 10 minutes or the last hour).
4. Leave **Record session replay** on and click **New Capture**.

The browser picks the capture up at its next poll, within 5 minutes, or when the tab becomes visible again after its polling interval; `refreshConfig()` polls at once. While a capture runs, the browser polls every minute. Each tab uploads the lookback once per capture, then streams until the capture ends or someone cancels it in the **Captures** list. A project can have 20 captures running at once, and a capture never turns on what the app's ceilings turned off.
