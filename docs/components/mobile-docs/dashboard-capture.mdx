When one user reports a problem, record that user's next sessions without a release. A capture uploads what the device holds from its lookback, then streams breadcrumbs and replay until it expires.

1. Make the user findable: set the `user.id` attribute once they sign in (code above). A capture can also target one installation by its instance id, or one session.
2. Start the capture in one of three places:
   - on a session page, **Capture this device** (or **Capture this session**);
   - on an issue's event, **Capture device**, **Capture user** or **Capture session**;
   - on the **Data Collection** page, **New Capture**, for any attribute value.
3. In the **New Capture** dialog, check the **Target** and **Value**, pick how long it runs (**For**: 15 minutes to 24 hours) and how much of the past to **Include** (**Nothing earlier**, **Last minute**, **Last 10 minutes** or **Last hour**), leave **Record session replay** on, and click **New Capture**.

A capture lasts at most 24 hours, and a project has at most 20 active at a time. The **Captures** list on the **Data Collection** page shows each one's progress, from delivered to the device to data received; cancel one there. Its lookback is uploaded once per installation, and the records sent while it runs carry its id (`traceway.capture.ids`). Replay uploaded for it counts in the session's replay budget, like error clips.
