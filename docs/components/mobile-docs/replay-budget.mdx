- **Budget.** A session uploads at most 50 MiB and 10,000 events of replay, across its error clips and captures, and at most 4 MiB of pictures. Past that, the SDK stops uploading replay for the session and reports it in a `traceway.collection.health` record.
- **Destination.** Replay always goes to Traceway. With no token (standalone), nothing is recorded.
- **Wireframe, not pixels.** The wireframe rebuilds what the app shows as HTML in the browser. Text is drawn in the browser's font, so lines may break a little differently, and views that draw themselves are outlines.
