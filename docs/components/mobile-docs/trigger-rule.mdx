A recording trigger waits for a journey, a sequence of steps, and records the session when it happens: it uploads the replay and breadcrumbs the device holds from before the first step, then keeps recording for as long as you say. Build one on the dashboard:

1. Open **Data Collection** in the project's sidebar, click **New Rule** (or edit a rule) and open the **Triggers** tab.
2. Click **Add recording trigger** and give it a **Trigger name**.
3. For each step, pick its **Event** and fill in what it matches: a **View selector** (or **Widget selector** in Flutter) for a tap, a **Route pattern** such as `/orders/{id}` for a navigation, or a **Log message** for a log line. **Within seconds of previous step** limits how long the user may take. **Add step** adds the next one.
4. Set **Replay lookback (seconds)**, how much of what happened before the journey to upload, and **Continue recording (seconds)**, how long to stream after it. **Cooldown (seconds)** and **Captures per session** keep a journey the user repeats from recording again and again.
5. Save the rule.

Steps match in order, and events in between that match no step are ignored. Progress is kept on the device, so a journey still counts when the app restarts within the session. When a trigger fires, the session lists it on the **Sessions** page as a recording reason, with the steps that matched.
